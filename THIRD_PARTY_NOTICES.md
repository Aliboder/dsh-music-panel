## AutoEQ equalization profiles

- Project: https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq
- License: MIT (Copyright 2018-2022 Jaakko Pasanen)
- Data: `lib/data/autoeq-presets.json` is generated by
  `scripts/import-autoeq.mjs` from the project's `results/**/* FixedBandEQ.txt`
  files. Only the ten band gains and the preamp of each profile are stored.
- Measurements underlying those profiles come from third-party databases with
  their own terms — oratory1990, Crinacle, Rtings, Super Review, HypetheSonics,
  Innerfidelity, Reference Audio Analyzer and others. Each row keeps its source
  label (`source` column in the app) so a correction can be traced back to the
  measurement it came from.
- The profiles are target corrections generated against a Harman-style target,
  not official settings published by the headphone manufacturers.
